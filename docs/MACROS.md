# Battle macros

[Русский](RU.md#изменения-0999-dev)

Open **Macros / Связки**, immediately below **Loadouts / Комплекты**. Semantic Groups and Loadouts are required; Effects, Reference Cards and Controls remain optional. There are **four groups, eight presets per group, six action rows per preset**. Name each preset and choose its digit (0–9, unique within its group). Empty defaults use consecutive digits.

Select a row, then V/B/M/N, a semantic group or the full available list. Every choice page has at most ten rows, labelled **1–9, 0**; press the displayed digit or confirm the highlighted row. Q/E or Left/Right changes pages; F7 inspects an action when Reference Cards is installed. Weapon techniques record their source; N rows record a normal attack, including special normal-attack IDs. Guard is a separate row type. Friendly actions default to the hero; change their target in the row menu. **Save** keeps the draft; leaving the preset discards unsaved edits.

A preset's **Executor / Исполнитель** is available only when its last action that spends a slot is an **item or guard**. Choose an owned weapon or clear the choice. The weapon switches immediately before that paid row; instant rows before it retain their position. It does not change the separate N nomination. Normal attacks and skill rows retain their normal source/executor rules; this setting cannot replace their weapon. Trailing instant items do not become the turn's final paid action.

In battle, press **C → group digit → preset digit** (or confirm each highlighted choice). Empty groups and presets are hidden. C/Esc goes back one level, then closes. H can inspect effects and return to the same picker. Controls can remap the opening key. Presets display **paid actions, ОМ (MP), and КД (cooldown)**. `КД готово / 5` means no current cooldown blocks a row and the largest listed skill cooldown is five; `ждать 2` means a referenced skill still has two cooldown units. MP ranges include supported prior states and sources; `~` marks a fallback estimate when a transition cannot be verified.

A preset is unavailable if it exceeds remaining paid slots or a source, item stock or current cooldown check fails. One chosen enemy is shared by single-target hostile rows. Area/random actions use native targeting; friendly targets are recorded per row. Actual costs, cooldowns, stock and per-turn limits are checked again at execution. A later failure stops remaining rows of that preset; it does not refund completed effects or alter independent manual actions.

Paid rows enter the ordinary action queue. Instant rows run at their recorded positions, including between paid actions or after the last one. A preset containing only instant rows runs once without consuming a paid slot. Starting weapons still apply once at battle start. The separate final N nomination follows the existing policy: a sole weapon technique keeps its source; eligible multi-action rows can use the final nomination. Selected normal-attack IDs are retained.

While still entering a turn, **press Esc twice within 0.55 seconds at the battle command menu** to clear all unexecuted choices and restart at the first existing slot. Slot count is preserved without rerolling actions. Previously used instant items, paid resources and actual effects remain. Executed or already committed turns cannot be undone. Queue lines show MP costs beside selected skills. **Space additionally confirms**, while Enter and a remapped primary confirm keep working; the alternative key is configurable too.

Damage previews now traverse queued buffs and random target draws instead of estimating random shots from the current actor alone. Repeated hits can propagate supported state/buff effects between hits. Random attacks show the expected total; multi-enemy N random attacks also show compact per-enemy values. Unsupported formulas, resource-dependent hit chains or more than 64 distinct probability branches still produce an explicit unknown. Values are approximate expectations before target HP caps, with hit/critical chances and variance; they are not a guarantee of the observed roll.

Profiles are bounded local text, separate from game saves. Format-1 presets are interpreted as group one; the verified updater converts their section headers to format two and stores the original profile in its rollback directory. Names, digits, row order, sources and targets are retained. These personal files are never part of the public package.
